Evidence room

Look inside the claim.

Research House does not stop at conclusions. Each complete record descends into what was done, who participated, how evidence was analyzed, and what the study cannot establish.

01

Question and prediction

What was being tested, and what outcomes should follow if the account is useful?

02

Design and sample

Who or what was studied, under which conditions, with what exclusions and comparisons?

03

Measures and analysis

How were observations transformed into variables, models, tests, intervals, and effect estimates?

04

Results

What pattern was observed, including uncertainty, null findings, irregularities, and dependence among observations?

05

Interpretation

What do the authors conclude, and how far does that conclusion travel beyond the manipulation?

06

Limits and alternatives

Which confounds, competing explanations, measurement choices, and replication questions remain?

Reading rule

Match the claim to the record.

An experiment, secondary dataset, simulation, and theoretical synthesis answer different questions. Research House labels the record type so an elegant idea is not mistaken for a direct empirical test.
